03 / A little introduction

About me.

My life is an intersection of grids. Through words, I collect memories. Through design, I create experiences.

I'm Amoolia, a designer, writer, and curious observer of the things that make up everyday life.

Writing, photography and design are my primary languages of expression. Each offers me a different way of observing, interpreting and making sense of the world. My work moves between visual communication, storytelling, research, and the spaces where these disciplines meet.

This archive is a collection of things I've made, questions I've asked, ideas I've followed, and things I haven't quite figured out yet.
