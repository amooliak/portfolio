02. Seller Dashboards

Client: Ebay

Role: Product Designer

Sellers were struggling to keep track of multiple issues across different orders, systems and webpages. The experience felt fragmented and made it difficult to understand costs, actions, and order status clearly.

eBay sellers managed refunds, damaged goods, shipment delays, and customer issues across multiple pages and disconnected workflows. Tracking the status of post-purchase issues was time-consuming, making it difficult to understand what required immediate attention and increasing the effort needed to resolve customer requests.

Research revealed that sellers weren't struggling to complete individual tasks—they were struggling to maintain visibility across many orders at once. They needed a single place to monitor issue status, prioritize actions, and understand the financial impact of each case without navigating between multiple systems.

I simplified the workflow by organizing seller issues into a clearer, centralized system. Used visual hierarchy and tracking states to help sellers prioritize actions faster.

Along with the seller onboarding experience on eBay, I designed a site builder that helps sellers quickly createand manage their storefronts. I reimagined how a seller page should function by introducing automated templates, making it easier to set up, customize, and maintain a consistent, professional presence.
