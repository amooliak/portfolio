Blue Poem

Published in: Arohana · Read time: 1 min

A poem about the colour Blue

to the world, blue is cold. it is the colour of distance, winter, and the mouth of the ocean. blue belongs to the faraway. but i have never known blue that way. blue is warmth to me. i see blue and my nerves settle into their place. i have always found warmth in what other people call cold.
